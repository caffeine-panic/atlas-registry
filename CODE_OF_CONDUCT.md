# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Atlas Registry welcomes contributors and users of every background and experience level. Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent while discussing ideas, and focus criticism on code, behavior, and evidence rather than people.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- giving actionable technical feedback;
- respecting privacy and avoiding production data in reports;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- helping newcomers understand the project's safety constraints.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents Atlas Registry.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and explain the violated standard when doing so is safe.

For behavior on GitHub, use GitHub's report/blocking tools and notify the repository maintainers without repeating harmful or private content publicly. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
